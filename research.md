### Biology education research (current)

As a postdoctoral researcher in discipline-based education research (DBER), I study how undergraduates learn core biological ideas and scientific practices. Current projects examine how students reason about evolution and animal behavior, and how course design shapes their ability to interpret data.

**Methods:** classroom-based studies, concept inventories and assessments, interviews, mixed-methods analysis.
